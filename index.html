Range 30.000 to 125.875 Ω in 0.125 Ω steps, 768 values with no gaps. Total = 30 + (0 or 32) + 8a + 1b + 0.125c, where a, b and c are the positions of the leftmost closed relay in R3, R2 and R1, counted from 0.

In each ladder row only the leftmost closed relay carries current. A row with no closed relay breaks the circuit, so with every relay off at power-up the channel reads open.

In R4, R4-2 bypasses the 32 Ω. To add the 32 Ω, close R4-1 and open R4-2. TAT_INPUT_2 is the same node as TAT_INPUT_3.
